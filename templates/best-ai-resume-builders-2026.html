Best AI Resume Builders in 2026

Create a professional resume in minutes using the latest AI-powered resume builders.

1. Kickresume

Kickresume uses AI to generate professional resumes, cover letters, and job-ready templates quickly.

2. Resume.io

Resume.io offers modern resume templates, AI writing suggestions, and easy customization.

3. Rezi AI

Rezi helps optimize resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), increasing interview chances.

4. Canva Resume Builder

Canva provides attractive resume templates that can be edited with AI writing assistance.

5. Zety

Zety guides users step-by-step while using AI recommendations to improve resume quality.

Conclusion

AI resume builders save time and help job seekers create professional resumes that stand out. Choosing the right tool can improve your chances of landing interviews.
